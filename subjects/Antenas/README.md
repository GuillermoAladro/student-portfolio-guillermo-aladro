# Antennas & Electromagnetic Compatibility

This subject studies the principles, analysis methods and design trade-offs of representative antenna families. The practices progress from wire radiators to printed and aperture antennas, documenting geometry, impedance, radiation patterns, gain and directivity.

## Laboratory work

1. [Wire Antenna Analysis](<Practice_1_Wire_Antenna_Analysis_English_Final.pdf>) — fundamental wire radiators and their electromagnetic behaviour.
2. [Advanced Wire Antennas](<Practice_2_Wire_Antennas_English_Only_Final_v2 (1).pdf>) — extended configurations and performance comparison.
3. [Microstrip Antennas](<Practice_3_Microstrip_Antennas_English_Only_Final_v2 (1).pdf>) — printed antenna structure, feeding and design constraints.
4. [Aperture Antennas](<Practice_4_Aperture_Antennas_English_Only_Final_v2 (1).pdf>) — aperture radiation and directional characteristics.

The accompanying [technical explanation](Explanation_Antennas_and_EMC_Practices.txt) summarizes the purpose and engineering context of the four practices.
